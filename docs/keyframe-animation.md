# Keyframe animation

## What it is

Data-driven animation for mobs whose client model plays authored keyframe tracks (rabbit, frog, bat, camel, armadillo, sniffer, and the baby rabbit, camel, armadillo, fox and axolotl). Definitions are transcribed as data, a per-model rig table says what plays when, and the shell keeps the per-entity timers the client derives from wire facts.

## How it works

1. **Definitions** (`lodestone_assets::keyframe`). An `AnimDef` is a length, a loop flag and channels of `Key`s per bone and `Target` (position, rotation, scale). `data.rs` is generated by `scripts/gen-keyframes.py` from the client's definition sources and checks the keyframe count. `ChannelDef::sample` returns an offset added to the part's pose (positions and rotations add, scale adds `value - 1`): halving search for the segment, next key's interpolation (`Linear` or `CatmullRom` over four keys, clamped), times a weight. `AnimDef::clock_seconds` wraps looping clocks.
2. **Rig tables** (`lodestone_render::entity_keyframe`). `rig_spec(model_name)` returns a `RigSpec`: head-tracking rule, walk cycles keyed on limb swing (`WalkSpec`), state animations keyed on a `Slot`, and parts hidden by state. `KeyframeRig::resolve` binds bone names to part indices at mesh build. `Skeleton::setup_anim` calls `KeyframeRig::apply`: the rig replaces the family limb animation, and the head is assigned, not added. Bone `root` is the unnamed root part. Hidden parts are zero-scaled after composing so children stay put. A keyframed mesh pads its culling box by `KEYFRAME_BOUNDS_PAD`.
3. **Input** (`Keyframes` in `AnimInput`). Per slot, milliseconds since start or stop, plus flags `Swimming`, `Hiding`, `Searching`, `Resting`.
4. **Timers** (`lodestone::entities::KeyframeTimers`, a component only on the seven species). `tick_keyframe_timers` runs in `TickSet::Animate` before the walk animation, fed from the ingest entity via `EntityIndex`. `KeyframeTimers::keyframes(partial_tick)` converts counters to render input; `tick_walk_animation` applies `walk_profile()` overrides (frog and camel scale and smooth differently).

Per species:

- **Rabbit**: entity event 1 starts a 15-tick jump; the hop plays while the counter is positive; a random 180-220 tick timeout starts an idle head tilt, suppressed while hopping or leashed.
- **Bat**: bit 0 of the flags byte is roosting.
- **Frog**: a pose change starts the jump, croak (pose 8) or tongue (pose 9) and stops the others; swim and idle bob follow a water probe at the feet; walk is zeroed while jumping.
- **Camel**: the signed pose-change stamp (negative = sitting) against `lodestone_ecs::GameClock` gives sit, held sit and stand windows; dash comes from its flag. A dash flag change after the first tick restarts a 55-tick cooldown (unless running) that nods the head: `Keyframes::pitch_bump` is `45 * cooldown / 55` degrees; `Track::Bumped` clamps look pitch to `-25..45`, adds the nod and clamps to `-25..70`. The rider seat uses the same stamp ([entity-physics](entity-physics.md)).
- **Armadillo**: state ordinal drives roll, unroll, peek; the shell parts hide on the reference schedule while the ball shows; event 64 restarts a peek, a scared state fast-forwards.
- **Sniffer**: each state change stops all five animations and starts the named one; searching switches the walk cycle.
- **Axolotl**: a baby solos one state per tick: playing dead, else moving (walking or look changed) which swims in open water, walks on ground or plays nothing, else idles in water, on the floor under water, or on dry ground. In-water is a mid-body block probe; on-ground is the server's last report. Walk gates the limb cycle (`BabyAxolotlWalkFloor`, speed 15, scale 30) and plays the under-water walk loop by time beside it; the client's under-water walk state is never drawn. An adult keeps four factors (dead, water, ground, moving) counting ticks to 10 and eased by `(1 - cos(PI x)) / 2`; `KeyframeTimers::axolotl_factors` feeds `Posture::axolotl` ([entity-postures](entity-postures.md)).

Wire facts: the camel, bat, sniffer and axolotl rows in `packets/metadata/appearance.rs`, and `lodestone_ecs::entity::StatusEvents`, filled by `apply_entity_status` for `STATUS_ANIMATED_KINDS` only.

## How to change it

- New animation: add its constant to `WANTED` in `scripts/gen-keyframes.py`, run it, then `scripts/keyframe-oracle.py` to rewrite the fixture. Never edit `data.rs`.
- New model: add a `RigSpec` and a `rig_spec` row; add the model to `every_rig_bone_exists_in_its_model`.
- New species timers: a `Species`, a `step_*` function and a case in `Species::of`. Wire-event starts need the kind in `STATUS_ANIMATED_KINDS`. A new wire field needs a `MobAppearance` field and a metadata row ([entity-appearance-parity](entity-appearance-parity.md)).
- Gotchas: the keyframed rest pose is the model's authored one, so a head authored with a rest tilt (rabbit) must be overwritten by the head rule; the Bevy query tuples in `extract_entity_draws` and `tick_keyframe_timers` are at arity limits, so nest new items; timers run at 20 Hz and the draw adds the partial tick, so animation never leads its tick.
- The baby fox walk is keyframed but its head is not: the posture rig ([entity-postures](entity-postures.md)) runs afterward and assigns the head.

## Configuration

None. The generator reads reference sources under `.cache/mc/<mc-version>/client-src`.

## Dependencies

`lodestone-assets` (definitions, sampler), `lodestone-render` (rigs, skeleton), `lodestone-ecs` (`StatusEvents`, ingest), `lodestone-v26-2` (metadata decode, shared by 26.3), the shell's `EntityInterpPlugin`. Oracles: `crates/lodestone-assets/tests/entity/keyframe.rs` against `tests/fixtures/keyframe-samples.txt` (written by `scripts/keyframe-oracle.py`, an independent double-precision evaluator); rig checks in `crates/lodestone-render/tests/entities/keyframe_rig.rs`; wire-to-draw in `crates/lodestone-shell/tests/entities/keyframe_wire.rs`; camel nod and baby axolotl states against the real client's pose setup in `posture_oracle.rs`.
